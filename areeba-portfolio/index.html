3D Engine Modeling

SolidWorks 3D Modeling Assembly Design Engineering Drawings

Chassis System Design

AutoCAD Project

Developed a comprehensive 3D chassis model with detailed engineering drawings, focusing on structural integrity, weight distribution, and material selection. The design incorporated stress analysis and optimization for vehicular applications.

AutoCAD Structural Design Technical Drawing Stress Analysis

Hydraulic Braking System

Functional Prototype

Constructed a working hydraulic braking system prototype using cardboard, syringes, and tubing to demonstrate Pascal's Law and hydraulic force transmission. The project effectively visualized pressure distribution and braking efficiency in a scaled model.

Prototyping Hydraulics Pascal's Law Fluid Mechanics

Thermodynamics Demonstration

Engineered a plasma cannon using a modified lighter as an ionization source to demonstrate electrical discharge and plasma formation. The project explored high-voltage applications and energy conversion principles in a controlled laboratory setting.

Plasma Physics Energy Conversion Lab Safety High Voltage

Thermodynamics Project

Conducted comprehensive energy and entropy analysis of the Rolls-Royce Trent 1000 aircraft engine. The study focused on thermodynamic cycles, efficiency calculations, and performance optimization under various operating conditions.

Thermodynamics Engine Analysis Efficiency Calculations Performance Metrics

Intermediate Level Lab Project
