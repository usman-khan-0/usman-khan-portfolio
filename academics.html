University & Department Overview

Air University

A premier institution in Pakistan offering world-class education across multiple faculties including Engineering Sciences, Computing & AI, and Aerospace & Strategic Studies. The university is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and innovation.

PEC Accreditation - Level II

The BEME program is accredited by Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) at Level-II, which is equivalent to the Washington Accord – an international agreement for professional engineering education recognized by 21+ countries worldwide.

Department of Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering (DMAE)

The Department of Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering offers cutting-edge programs with focus on Industry 4.0 technologies, advanced research areas, and comprehensive laboratory facilities.

GPA Calculation

SGPA (Semester GPA) = Sum of (Grade Points × Credit Hours) / Total Credit Hours per semester

CGPA (Cumulative GPA) = Sum of all (Grade Points × Credit Hours) / Total Credit Hours to date
