GPA Calculation

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is calculated based on semester performance. CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is the weighted average of all completed semesters.

Formula: GPA = Σ(Course Grade × Credit Hours) / Σ(Credit Hours)
